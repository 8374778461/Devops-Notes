### Project: Integrated Eligibility System (IES)

The **Integrated Eligibility System**, or IES, is a backend application used to determine whether a person is eligible for different benefits or programs based on their personal, financial, and eligibility information.

I worked as a **Python Backend Developer** on this project. My main responsibility was developing backend APIs, implementing business logic, working with the database, and fixing issues.

The application follows a typical **client → REST API → business logic → database** architecture.

For example, when a user submits an application, the request comes to our REST API. The backend validates the applicant's information and applies the required eligibility rules. Based on the result, the system determines whether the applicant is eligible, not eligible, or requires additional information.
