Label :

Label = Name Tag for a Pod

labels:
  app: java-app


Selector

Selector = Search Mechanism

selector:
  app: java-app

"Find all Pods having app=java-app"

Labels are key-value pairs attached to Pods for identification. Selectors are used by Kubernetes objects like Services, ReplicaSets, and Deployments to find and manage Pods based on those labels.
